# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. This code applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

- Be respectful and patient. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and make room for different perspectives.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about anyone.
- Keep conversations relevant and avoid spam or unwelcome contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a conversation, or restrict participation to protect contributors. They will consider context and severity, explain decisions when practical, and protect the privacy of people involved.

For a conduct concern, contact the [repository owner](https://github.com/xxvw) through an available private contact channel. If no private channel is listed, ask for one in the repository without disclosing the incident or anyone's personal information. Reports made in good faith must not result in retaliation.

For software vulnerabilities, follow the [security reporting policy](SECURITY.md).
